---
title: "for you"
date: 2026-07-07
---

I would die for you.  

I'd throw myself into fire  
To fetch flowers you admire  
I'd carve your name into my skin  
With a poisonous and rusty pin  

You'd cancel our first date  
Throw away the gifts I made  
Leave my messages unread  
Forget me on an empty street  

I'd swallow acid, turn to dust,  
Walk on nails and broken glass  
I'd throw myself from moving car,  
I'd fight for you a hopeless war  

You'd cover our pictures gray,  
You'd forget about my birthday  
You'd steal my jacket for the night  
Without ever saying "thanks" or "bye"  

I'd pay for your every cheque,  
I'd kiss you gently on the neck  
Hold you tightly to my chest  
Keep the guard when you rest  

You'd betray me on first chance  
Dismiss me on quick glance  
Punch me in my guts  
Let me die by thousand cuts  

I can only wish that  
You could be for somebody  
What I would be for you
